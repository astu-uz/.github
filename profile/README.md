# Tashkent Regional Branch of Astrakhan State Technical University

**Branch of the Federal State Budgetary Educational Institution of Higher Education "Astrakhan State Technical University" in the Tashkent region of the Republic of Uzbekistan**

Established in 2019 by Resolution No. 608 of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan. The branch trains specialists for Uzbekistan in:

- Ecology and environmental management
- Economics
- Food technology
- Water management
- Aquaculture

This organization hosts teaching materials, student projects and digital services of the branch. It is managed by the **Center for Digital Education Technologies**.
